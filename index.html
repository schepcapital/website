Systematic trading research

Can daily price and volume data deliver better risk-adjusted returns than the market?

I’m Reinier Schep. At Schep Capital, I use daily price and volume data to rank assets, decide how far to lean long or short, and size positions based on risk. I test how closely the portfolio should follow those target weights once trading costs are taken into account.

My goal is steady returns, a higher Sortino ratio than a broad market benchmark, and a maximum drawdown below 10%. I test the strategy on unseen data and evaluate its performance after trading costs.
