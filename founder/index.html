Founder

Reinier Schep

I started Schep Capital to research and build systematic investment strategies. I’m interested in how much you can learn from widely available market data when the research question is specific and the test is realistic.

My current focus is cross-sectional equity research. I’m working through the full path from a signal hypothesis to a portfolio, with particular attention to when the data was available, what could have been traded, and how much of the signal survives costs.
